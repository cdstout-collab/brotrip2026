Arrival note

There is no reception or luggage storage. Send the flight number, confirm the luggage-drop time, show passports, pay tourist tax, and collect keys.

Airport & Local Transportation

BCN → apartment

After passport control and customs, use an airport transfer or taxi to the apartment. The planning allowance is 30–40 minutes with carry-ons.

Saturday Fountain option

Use Metro L1/L3 to Plaça d’Espanya. Keep dinner close to Montjuïc for the 8:00 PM Fountain; verify the date-specific schedule before going.

Transit card

Buy a Hola BCN 96-hour or 120-hour card at the airport metro station or online. It covers unlimited metro/bus travel for five travelers.

Carcassonne

Planning sheet route: Barcelona Sants → Narbonne → Carcassonne by train, allowing about 3–3½ hours each way and one connection. Confirm the Dec. 1 timetable before booking.
